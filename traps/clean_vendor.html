Vendor renewals — Q4 summary

Three contracts are up for renewal this quarter: the monitoring platform (renew, 8% price increase negotiated down from 15%), the ticketing tool (renew for 12 months while we evaluate alternatives), and the legacy backup service (do not renew; migration to the new backup tier completes 20 November).

Total annual spend across the three falls by roughly 11% compared with last year. Finance has approved the monitoring and ticketing renewals.
