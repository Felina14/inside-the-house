On-call rotation: changes from 1 December

From 1 December the primary on-call rotation moves from weekly to four-day shifts (Monday–Thursday, Friday–Sunday handled by a separate weekend rota). This follows feedback from the October survey, where 71% of respondents said seven-day shifts were too long.

Secondary on-call stays weekly. Paging thresholds are unchanged. Handover now happens at 10:00 local time rather than 09:00, so the outgoing engineer can finish the morning check.
